    Shot 03InsertinteractiveThe one distinction this skill never blurs.

    A zoom is not a dolly.

    Same subject, same final size. Watch the pillars. A zoom changes the focal length: it crops the view, and the space stays flat. A dolly moves the camera: parallax appears, and you enter the space. A dolly zoom does both at once. The subject stays the same size while the world bends around it.

    FOCAL 35 mmDISTANCE 6.0 mSUBJECT 40% of frameFOCUS subject
    "Zoom = the act of looking. Dolly = entering the space."
    Shot 04Trackingtruck rightWe follow the method, step by step.

    Eight steps from script to set.

    Not a list of tips. A decision engine. Each step writes one part of the report, and a checker validates the result before anything is shot or rendered.
