# Scoring rubric (0-4 per criterion)

4 = a professional DoP would sign it · 3 = good, small fixes · 2 = usable with real fixes · 1 = weak · 0 = missing/wrong.
Long output earns nothing. Score whether each decision improves the storytelling.

| # | Criterion | What a 4 looks like |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Narrative understanding | beats, turns and the reveal identified correctly; intent kept |
| 2 | Shot selection | sizes/angles serve each beat; variety with purpose |
| 3 | Movement motivation | every move has a gate motivation; static used where right |
| 4 | Zoom vs dolly | the right one chosen and named; no confusion; 2D scale called zoom |
| 5 | Focus direction | focus targets and racks timed and motivated; DOF budget kept |
| 6 | Composition | start/end frames designed; hierarchy clear; format zones respected |
| 7 | Lens | distance-first reasoning; focal lengths justified for the scene |
| 8 | Lighting | motivated, executable, follows the emotional arc |
| 9 | Colour consistency | one colour script; accent used with meaning |
| 10 | Continuity | geography, 180/30, eyelines, props; cdcheck 0 errors |
| 11 | Timestamp accuracy | times match the given word times; shots edge to edge |
| 12 | AI prompt quality | one shot per prompt, positive phrasing, continuity sheet, fallbacks (n/a if no AI) |
| 13 | Executability | a crew/tool could act without asking questions |
| 14 | Restraint | budgets kept; no effects for their own sake |
| 15 | Storytelling gain | the plan makes the story clearer/stronger than a plain reading |

Pass: mean >= 3.0, no criterion < 2, cdcheck 0 errors.
