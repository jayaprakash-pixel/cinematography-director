## 4. Visual strategy (CREATIVE)
**Core idea:** the film moves from **far to near**, then back out. The temples are seen far and wide, the way a visitor sees them. The small things are seen near and close, the way you remember them. At dusk the film goes wide again, but now the frame has "us" in it, and the places are empty and quiet.

**Information ledger**
| Beat | Viewer knows | Not yet |
|---|---|---|
| B1-B2 | We are somewhere with rain; it is Kyoto, temples | who "we" is |
| B3-B5 | there is a partner; the film cares about small details | the partner's face |
| B6-B7 | the partner's face, their laugh; the filmmaker is in the story too (S17) | - |
| B8-B12 | the day is ending | - |

**See / hide list**
| Hidden | Method | Reveal at | Reveal method |
|---|---|---|---|
| the rain (picture) | sound only over black; wide shots keep the rain small | 00:45.0 (S09) | ECU of drops on a step, on the word "rain" |
| the partner's face | from behind (S04, S05), hands only (S06, S08), silhouette against window (S13) | 01:27.0 (S16) | MCU, front, at the sip and the laugh |
| the filmmaker | behind the camera | 01:35.0 (S17) | phone 2-shot on the table |

**Subjects per beat:** B2 place (primary) / crowd (secondary); B3-B5 partner's back and hands / rain; B6-B8 partner's face / tea and steam; B9-B11 the city going quiet / the couple small in it.

**Motif register**
| Motif | First | Returns | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stone steps | S03 (crowded, day) | S09-S10 (close, rain), S24 (same position, empty, lanterns) | crowd -> detail -> empty and quiet |
| Partner's hands | S06 (water ladle) | S08 (catching rain), S18 (around the cup), S25 (held in the filmmaker's hand) | alone -> with us |
| Partner from behind | S04 (face hidden) | S23 (same follow; they turn and look back) | stranger-like -> known face |
| Pagoda wide | S02 (day, overcast) | S27 (blue hour, lit) | visitor's view -> memory's last frame |

**Colour script**
| Item | Choice |
|---|---|
| Base | the real colours of wet Kyoto in autumn: stone grey, moss green, overcast sky. Keep the maple red natural - do not push it (that is the "ad" look the brief rejects). |
| Motif colour | warm amber of tea, wood and lanterns. In this video, amber = closeness. It is small in the day act, fills the teahouse, and returns in the dusk lanterns. |
| Accent | none added. The motif colour does the job; an extra accent would read as a design choice, not a memory. |
| Progression | Act 1 (temples): cool, slightly desaturated (-10% sat.). Act 2 (rain + tea): cool outside, warm inside; the warmest frame is S16. Act 3 (dusk): blue ambient + amber practicals. |
| "Like remembering" look | lift blacks slightly (no pure black except S01/S28), soft highlight roll-off, fine grain. No heavy LUT, no teal-orange, no vignette. |
| Continuity | one white balance per scene group (section 8.2); skin tones matched across S16-S19. |

**Visual identity:** 27 mm-equiv. (18 mm) for places, 52 mm-equiv. (35 mm prime) for people and details. Eye level always (a memory sees from your own height); low only for the steps and the rain. Camera still by default; moves only when someone walks (4 follows/tilt in 28 shots). Natural light only. 16:9.

**Intended response:** Act 1: "it is beautiful, but it is everyone's Kyoto." Act 2: "this is *their* Kyoto." Act 3: "I am a little sad that it ended."

### 8.2 Camera settings and kit - TECHNICAL
| Item | Setting | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Format | UHD 3840x2160, 23.976p (fallback 25p if your region/camera only gives 25 - do not mix) | 2.0x punch-in headroom for 1080 delivery |
| Shutter | 1/50 (180° convention) | natural motion blur; rain reads as soft streaks |
| Profile | standard profile with contrast -1/-2, sharpness -1, or a 10-bit flat profile if available. Avoid 8-bit log. | 8-bit log bands in skies and steam |
| White balance | manual, fixed per scene: day outdoors 5600-6000 K (cloudy preset); teahouse 5600 K (window neutral, wood stays warm); dusk 4800-5000 K (sky stays blue, lanterns stay amber) | auto WB shifts inside a shot and erases the amber motif |
| ND | buy one variable ND (2-5 stops) for the 35 mm filter thread (and the kit lens if the threads differ) | overcast daylight at f/2.0, 1/50, ISO 100 is about 3-4 stops over |
| ND fallback | no ND: stop down to f/5.6-8 for places (deep is right there); for shallow details use shade or shoot them in the rain/indoors; last resort shutter 1/100-1/200 (rain becomes sharp drops - acceptable for S09 only) | - |
| Focus | AF-C with face/eye AF for people; manual focus for S09, S10, S20 (racks and drops) | AF hunts on rain and glass |
| Stabilisation | IBIS/lens OIS on for handheld; off on the gimbal if the gimbal manual says so | - |
| No tripod | brace on walls, railings, steps; a small bean bag or a folded scarf under the camera | static frames are the base of the film |
| Rain | rain cover or a plastic bag + lens hood; the partner holds the umbrella over the camera for S09-S10 | - |
| Gimbal | balance for the 18-55 at 18 mm; rebalance after lens changes | - |
| Phone | lock exposure/focus (AE/AF lock); same frame rate as the camera | matches the camera in the edit |
| Respect | check signs: some temple halls and gardens do not allow filming or gimbals; ask the teahouse; do not hold close on strangers' faces | personal film, real people |

## 9. Decisions explained

| D1 | **Split the VO** over its matching pictures (5 phrases at 00:04.8, 00:39.5, 00:46.0, 01:23.0, 02:21.0). | D2 | **Turn on a lens change** (S08: first 35 mm, first shallow frame) on "small things" (key beat B4). | The change in perspective and focus *is* the turn - from the visitor's wide view to the close view of memory. | A push-in from the steps to a raindrop: a move would add "ad" energy and needs a slider or a perfect gimbal walk. |
| D3 | **Withhold the partner's face until the tea** (S16, peak B7). Static MCU, 52 mm-equiv. at 1.2 m. | The laugh at the too-hot tea is the warmest moment; showing the face for the first time there makes it the peak without any camera effect. | A gimbal arc around the table: it would break the teahouse line of action and turn a private moment into a performance. |
| D4 | **Callbacks with a change** (S03->S24 steps, S02->S27 pagoda), same position and lens. | "The city goes quiet" is shown by comparison: the same frame, crowd -> empty, day -> lanterns. | New dusk locations: prettier, maybe, but the change would not read. |
