# T03 - Cinematic personal travel video (16:9, ~3 minutes)
Trip: 5 days in Kyoto in autumn with my partner. Gear: one mirrorless camera (APS-C), 18-55 mm kit zoom and a 35 mm f/1.8 prime, a small gimbal, my phone. No crew, no lights. I want it to feel calm and intimate, "like remembering it", not like an ad. I will record a short voice-over afterwards: "We came for the temples. We stayed for the small things: the rain on the steps, the tea that was too hot, the way the city goes quiet at dusk."
Plan what to shoot (I can't control events) and how to edit it. Music will be added later.
