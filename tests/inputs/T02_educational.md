# T02 - 7-minute educational YouTube video (16:9)
Title: "How a vaccine teaches your immune system" - for high-school students. Production: one presenter in a small studio (real camera, 1 camera operator, 2 LED panels, one slider), plus a 3D animator for 3 short animations, plus stock B-roll allowed. Edited in DaVinci Resolve.
Chapters (times approximate):
0:00-0:40 Hook: "Your body has a memory - and a vaccine writes the first entry." Presenter holds an old vaccination card.
0:40-2:00 Ch1 The invader: virus enters, multiplies (animation needed).
2:00-3:30 Ch2 The defenders: innate vs adaptive immunity; B cells, T cells, antibodies (animation).
3:30-5:00 Ch3 The rehearsal: what a vaccine contains; the immune system practises on a harmless piece (animation + presenter with a simple prop: a "wanted poster" of a virus).
5:00-6:20 Ch4 The real attack: memory cells respond fast; graph of antibody response first vs second exposure.
6:20-7:00 Close: back to the vaccination card; "every entry in this card is a lesson your body remembers."
Full text of the hook (0:00-0:40): "This card is 30 years old. It looks like paperwork. But each stamp on it is a memory - not in your head, in your blood. Today I'll show you how a tiny, harmless sample can teach billions of cells to recognise an enemy they have never met."
